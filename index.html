Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information regarding food ordering and meal reservations with Zee Homemade Food.

How do I place an order?

Select your preferred dish or dessert from our menu and fill out the order form, or click any WhatsApp button on the page. Your order request will be prepared and sent directly to us on WhatsApp for confirmation.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based at Dhoke Kala Khan, Rawalpindi, and serve customers across Rawalpindi and Islamabad.

Can I reserve meals in advance?

Yes, you can schedule meal requests for upcoming days by selecting your preferred date and time in the order form.

How is my order confirmed?

Once you submit your order details on WhatsApp, we verify kitchen availability and confirm the timing and details directly with you.
